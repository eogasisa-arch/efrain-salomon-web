Footprints Along the Way

From Purpose to Inner Awakening
A true story that transforms the way we understand love, time, and life itself.

An invitation to pause, look inward, and discover that what truly matters is not what we give... but how fully we are present.
“No one should have to lose what they love before understanding its true value.”
There are moments in life that force us to stop...
not because we choose to, but because we can no longer continue the same way.

This book was born from a real, profound, and life-changing experience. It tells the story of a father who, in his effort to provide security for his family, discovers—at the most unexpected moment—that what matters most is not material success, but the things that can never be recovered: time, presence, and shared love.

Through an experience that shakes the soul, Efraín Salomón III takes readers on an intimate journey where fear, guilt, faith, and hope intertwine, leading to a powerful inner transformation.

More than a book, it is a mirror.
An invitation to pause, reflect, and reconnect with what is truly important.
  • Reflections born from real-life experiences
  • Lessons about love, family, and purpose
  • A call to live with greater awareness and presence
This is not simply a book to be read...
it is a book that can change the way you choose to live.
